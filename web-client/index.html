MusicMute Web App

Remove background music. Keep the voice.

Use MusicMute in your browser to isolate vocals from audio you own or have permission to process. Sign in or create an account to begin.

From audio to your vocal library

  1. Upload a local audio file or import a supported source link.
  2. Follow the processing progress in your account.
  3. Listen to completed vocal tracks in your online library.

The interface supports English and Arabic. Source availability, account limits, and the original recording affect processing and results; complete music removal is not guaranteed.

The sign-in form opens when the app loads. JavaScript is required for sign-in, uploads, processing updates, and playback.
