# MusicMute listing draft

Draft dated 2026-09-10. Not published; not a statement of store approval. Actual public URLs, developer identity, monitored support contact and supported retention commitments are required before use.

## Short description

Create vocals-only audio from audio you own or have permission to use.

## Description

Import an audio file, review its name, size and duration, and choose Remove music to create vocals-only audio.

MusicMute prepares a private local copy before you decide to process it. Confirm that you own the audio or have permission to use it, then explicitly start cloud processing. Follow the processing status in your account library. When a result is ready, tap to play it, download it, save a copy or share it.

Processing uses a remote service and requires uploading your confirmed audio. Results are vocals-only MP3 audio. MusicMute does not provide an instrumental output or claim perfect separation for every recording.

Supported inputs include MP3, M4A/MP4 audio, AAC, OGG and WebM, subject to media validation. Inputs must be nonempty, smaller than 30,000,000 bytes and shorter than 600 seconds. Do not advertise WAV or FLAC support without verifying both clients and the complete pipeline.

A clearly labelled secondary YouTube link option is available. Download only when you have permission and the source service permits it. Confirm source downloading explicitly, then review the downloaded audio separately before cloud processing. Rights confirmation does not verify copyright ownership or grant third-party permissions.

Manage account settings and request deletion from the app. An external support-assisted route is also provided once the public pages and monitored mailbox are configured and verified. Original files you imported and copies you exported remain under your control.

## Excluded claims

No entirely offline processing, automatic copyright verification, unlimited third-party download permission, instrumental stems, guaranteed quality, instant deletion of all backups, fixed completion deadline, or store-approval guarantee is claimed.

The public deletion resource must work without reinstalling the app. A customer-support email can provide an actionable route, but the workflow must actually operate before submission. See [Google Play account deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en), checked 2026-09-10.

The retained downloader still needs an independent distribution and rights review. Permission guidance is not a policy exemption. See [Google Play Intellectual Property](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072?hl=en), checked 2026-09-10.
