Keep the voice. Reduce the music.

MusicMute separates voice from background music in audio you own or have permission to process. Choose a local audio or video file, review the prepared audio, confirm your rights, and submit it for cloud processing.

YOUR AUDIO LIBRARY
Follow processing progress, keep your results in an account library, compare the prepared original with the voice-only result, and save audio to your device. Play completed audio in the background using Android media controls.

ENGLISH AND ARABIC
Use MusicMute in English or Arabic, including right-to-left layouts. Account settings provide privacy information, support, and account deletion controls.

BEFORE YOU START
An account and internet connection are required for cloud processing. Processing time, availability, and limits depend on service capacity and account policy. Voice separation quality varies with the source audio; background music may not be removed completely.

Only process media you own or are authorized to use. MusicMute does not grant rights to third-party content. Review the privacy policy for information about audio processing, service providers, retention, and deletion.
