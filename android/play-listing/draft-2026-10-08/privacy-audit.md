## Analytics purposes

Keep **Analytics** for **App interactions**, **User IDs**, **Crash logs**, and **Diagnostics**. Add **Analytics** to **Device or other IDs** in the draft.

- [Admin overview](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/admin-observability/admin-overview.service.ts:172) aggregates job submissions, unique submitting `userId` values, daily submissions, completion/failure/cancellation counts, and queue/processing timings. Job submissions and their statuses provide a concrete first-party use/performance analytics basis for App interactions. This basis does not depend on collecting page views or using an analytics SDK.
- [Device statistics](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/operations/device-statistics.ts:52) aggregates retained device records by last-seen time, platform, app version and build, and counts installations and distinct users. The [operations CLI](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/operations/cli.ts:240) exposes this report. Installation identifiers and User IDs are therefore used for analytics as well as device/account functionality.
- [Client error records](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/client-errors/client-error.schema.ts:16) retain owner ID, failure stage/code, versions, HTTP status and timestamps for 30 days. These are Diagnostics and owner-linked User IDs. They should not be treated as generic UI interaction telemetry.
- [Android Sentry filtering](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/android/app/src/main/java/com/hatem/musicmute/SentryMonitoring.kt:14) disables breadcrumbs, tracing, automatic sessions, screenshots and view hierarchy, and strips user/request/context content. [Backend filtering](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/observability/sentry.ts:44) disables automatic integrations and limits transmitted failure data. These restrictions do not erase the separate first-party analytics above.

Google's Analytics purpose includes measuring app usage and performance and diagnosing failures. Its service-provider sharing exception requires processing on the developer's behalf and instructions. Neither having a vendor SDK nor pseudonymizing an identifier alone establishes that exception. [Google Play Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469).

## IP-derived location

[Location parsing](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/devices/cloudflare-location.ts:54) accepts Cloudflare country, continent, region, city, postal/metro codes, timezone, latitude and longitude. [Device persistence](/Users/hatemragap/work_spaces/music_remover/backend/src/devices/device.schema.ts:27) retains these values and an observation timestamp. **Approximate location is collected and is not ephemeral.** App functionality and account management are source-backed purposes.

[Google's location taxonomy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469) classifies location by area: approximate is at least 3 km²; precise is less than 3 km². Inferred IP location is included in both categories. Current [Cloudflare IP geolocation documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/network/ip-geolocation/) describes the result as an estimate without an accuracy SLA. The [latitude](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ruleset-engine/rules-language/fields/reference/ip.src.lat/) and [longitude](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ruleset-engine/rules-language/fields/reference/ip.src.lon/) references do not give an accuracy area. Decimal precision in the source validator is not physical accuracy. Public documentation therefore does **not** prove a categorical exclusion of Precise location; retain that answer as unresolved until the coordinate accuracy scope is established.
