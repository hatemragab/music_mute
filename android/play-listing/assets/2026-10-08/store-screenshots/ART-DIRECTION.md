# MusicMute Play screenshot art direction

## Solo Signal Studio

The screenshot series treats the isolated voice as a signal emerging from a
quiet studio. Deep green fields connect the artwork to the MusicMute icon,
while the app itself stays grounded in its real near-black Android interface.
Mint waveforms identify the voice and warm orange marks the action that moves a
track forward.

Each image has one short promise and one faithful product scene. The headline
uses generous space, then the device carries the detail: file import, visible
processing progress, original-versus-voice playback, or the processed Library.
The copy never promises perfect separation, speed, offline processing, or
unlimited use.

The phone series uses a tall device with the existing bottom navigation. The
7-inch tablet series uses the Android wide layout: a navigation rail for Home
and Library, centered content, and focused full-screen processing and player
views. Both series share the same four-step story and exact 9:16 export ratio.

The compositions use generic, rights-clear sample names and contain no account,
reviewer, device, or personal data. Arabic exports mirror the same art direction
with native RTL alignment and localized interface labels.

## Visual rules

- Brand field: deep green `#123C37` with mint `#96E6C7` signal accents.
- Product surface: Android theme colors from `Theme.kt` and `AccentPalette.kt`.
- Action color: MusicMute Android default orange `#FF814A`.
- Typography: local system sans serif only; no remote fonts or dependencies.
- Output: four PNGs per locale and device group, RGB/RGBA, under 8 MB each.
- Phone: 1080 x 1920. 7-inch tablet: 1440 x 2560. Both are exact 9:16.
