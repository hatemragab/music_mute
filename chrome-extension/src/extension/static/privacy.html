Privacy and data use

MusicMute Local · Updated October 4, 2026

What the extension reads

On supported YouTube watch pages, MusicMute reads the video identifier, duration, playback position, volume, mute state and player/ad state to prepare and synchronize vocals. It does not read your general browsing history, Google password or browser cookies.

What stays on your Mac

The extension stores playback preferences and a bounded local diagnostic history in Chrome. The required MusicMute Mac app acquires audio online and stores reusable results in a local cache. The extension follows the processing choice saved in the app: local separation or MusicMute cloud. Playback uses a temporary, protected connection to the app on this Mac.

Cloud processing

When MusicMute cloud is selected in the app, manually starting a video sends the acquired audio to MusicMute using the saved choice, without a per-video Chrome confirmation. Cloud processing requires the app's signed-in account and uses its processing allowance. Vocals are downloaded to the Mac for synchronized playback. Automatic preparation never submits new cloud processing. Account credentials remain in the Mac app and are not sent to Chrome.

The downloader runs as a logged-out guest without importing your Chrome account or cookies. YouTube and its media servers receive the network requests needed to acquire the selected audio, including your IP address. Guest access may be refused; MusicMute does not bypass login requirements.

Signing into the Mac app

A MusicMute account is optional for local playback. When you sign into the companion app, new local results can be staged for saving to your account, including original audio, vocals and associated video metadata. Account saving uses MusicMute services and private cloud storage, subject to your account's access and storage limits. Local processing does not mean that signed-in results always remain only on your device.

Your controls

You can turn automatic preparation on or off in the video controls, set a duration limit, and stop playback at any time. Stop ends playback; it does not delete previously saved results.

Use “Stop playback and clear local cache” in the extension's local tools to clear its managed reusable results. Pending account saves and account Library items are managed separately in the Mac app. Removing the extension removes its Chrome settings, but does not uninstall the Mac app or delete its files or cloud Library.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics are saved locally. MusicMute does not automatically send diagnostic reports, telemetry or analytics from this extension. Exporting diagnostics creates a local report; you choose whether to share it. Reports omit audio, credentials, browser cookies and private media URLs.

Purpose and sharing

Data is used to provide voice playback, selected processing and account saving when signed in. This extension does not sell data, use it for advertising, or send it to analytics services. It is an independent MusicMute product and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
